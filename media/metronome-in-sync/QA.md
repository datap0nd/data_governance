# In Sync review evidence

The [quiet presentation MP4](renders/review/animatic.mp4) is the current review artifact. It was rendered on 26 September 2026 at 1920×1080, 60 fps, for exactly 84 seconds. The encoded audio measured −20.20 LUFS integrated and −6.30 dBTP. The film preserves the silent pivot before the Metronome turn and the final black frame.

The six [encoded-frame samples](renders/review/encoded-samples.jpg) and eight individual scene frames show the manual handoffs, five-stage automation, overnight run history, AI evidence, sourced report and flat role illustrations. The film's original render used HyperFrames 0.8.78; the editable repository package pins 0.8.81 after a validated upgrade. The MP4 is an existing reviewed artifact and was not rerendered during repository import.

Repository-import validation and the associated test report are in [the release package](../../../docs/testing/releases/2026-09-28-in-sync-film/test-report.md). The final GitHub PR records its tested head and CI run.

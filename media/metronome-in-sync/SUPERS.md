- Keep every supplied line verbatim, including punctuation and the em dash. The source's general seven-word limit is a style target; exact supplied copy wins. “AI finds the answer — and shows its sources.” has eight words. The disclosure also has eight words when the separator is excluded.
- The six supplied kinetic words start at 15.5, 16.0, 16.5, 17.0, 17.5 and 18.0 seconds. The source does not specify words for the remaining starts through 19.5. The proposed fill is **Export. / Repeat. / Export.** at 18.5 / 19.0 / 19.5. These are deliberately brief, one-beat inserts, an explicit exception to the 1.5-second headline hold.
- The disclosure preserves its supplied 82.5-second start. The required last-beat black cut is 83.5 seconds, so its hold is **1.0 second**, not 1.5 seconds. For a full 1.5-second hold it must start at 82.0 seconds. The timeline preserves the supplied start pending owner feedback at this gate.
- The closing message remains on screen with the logo lockup until the cut. This makes it readable for 4.5 seconds while the end card builds.
- `HERO_STAT` is empty. No additional headline number is invented.

# Assets in the current film

The current cut uses code-drawn graphics and fictional interface data. Source systems appear as distinct dark windows, while a single physical metronome and the Metronome mark represent the platform. The overnight city and four role illustrations are SVG/HTML in `scripts/build.mjs`; no generated photograph appears in the committed composition.

- `fonts/outfit-300.ttf` through `outfit-800.ttf` are copied from this repository's `app/static/fonts/` for a self-contained film review. The film uses the existing application typeface.
- `vendor/gsap.min.js` is the GSAP 3.14.2 browser runtime. The npm lock records GSAP, Three.js, esbuild and HyperFrames versions.
- `audio/score.wav` is deterministic synthesized audio produced by `audio/score.py` with NumPy and FFmpeg. The committed MP4 includes its encoded AAC track.
- `build-data.json`, `film-runtime.js` and `compositions/frames/` are generated by `node scripts/build.mjs` and committed so the composition can be inspected immediately.
- Historical oscillator data and photographed-screen corner measurements are omitted. The current film does not display source metronomes or photographed screens.

The review MP4 and frames use illustrative report values. They do not contain live portal captures or raw business reports.

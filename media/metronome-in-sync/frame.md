# Film composition

1920×1080, 30 fps, 84 seconds. Light mode. Four scenes, timed by `timeline.json`, word cues in `assets/cues.json`, screen clips and camera moves in `assets/screen.json`, and one deterministic draw function. No narration captions.

The drawn scenes use Wizard's look: Inter, warm white surfaces, a cobalt accent (#315bd6), green only for "checked" and security, a soft cobalt and violet background glow. Each source has a consistent colour and letter badge (ASAP blue, GSCM violet, BDP teal, NERP rust, Excel green, Email blue, PowerPoint orange, PDF red); these are generic badges, not product logos. Wizard's own pixel logo, in Samsung Blue (#1428A0), is the hub and the end lockup, scaled by whole numbers so it stays crisp.

From 0:33 the screen is the real Wizard app (1600 × 900 page, recorded at 2×) in a rounded window. A camera keeps a page point at the frame centre at a given zoom and never shows the window's edge once it is wider than the frame. The first window grows out of the hub logo; the second question slides in from the right and the check slides back from the left. A dark "Timelapse" pill shows the real elapsed run time while a run plays sped up, and a small "Real Wizard app · illustrative data" tag stays in the top-right corner. Everything on the screens comes from Wizard's synthetic fixtures; no live service is accessed.

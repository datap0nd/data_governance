# Film composition

1920×1080, 30 fps, 122 seconds. Light mode. Four scenes, timed by `timeline.json`, word cues in `assets/cues.json`, screen clips and camera moves in `assets/screen.json`, and one deterministic draw function. No narration captions; the hunt's question and the closing statement are shown as titles.

The drawn scenes use Wizard's look: Inter, warm white surfaces, a cobalt accent (#315bd6), green only for "checked" and security, a soft cobalt and violet background glow. Each source has a consistent colour and letter badge (ASAP blue, GSCM violet, BDP teal, NERP rust, Excel green, Email blue, PowerPoint orange, PDF red); these are generic badges, not product logos. Wizard's own pixel logo, in Samsung Blue (#1428A0), is the hub and the end lockup, scaled by whole numbers so it stays crisp.

From 0:33 the screen is the real Wizard app (1600 × 900 page, its screencast frames scaled up 2× once) in a rounded window. The camera has a few keyframes per clip, each a page point at the frame centre and a zoom, eased between them without stopping or overshooting; in close-up it never shows the window's edge. It moves slowly and seldom, and holds while the page scrolls. While Wizard works, its stage holds still: each frame is moved by however far Wizard's own auto-scroll moved it. The first window grows out of the hub logo; the second question cross-fades in where the first was, and its check runs in that same window. The screens play in real time except while Wizard is working: then a dark "Timelapse" pill at the top centre shows how many times faster it plays (2× for question 1 and the check, 4× for question 2) and the real run time counting at that speed, and a blue edge frames the picture, for exactly that stretch. A small "Real Wizard app · illustrative data" tag stays in the top-right corner. Everything on the screens comes from Wizard's synthetic fixtures; no live service is accessed.

Motion follows current product films: anything that moves fast blurs along its path (a directional Gaussian per element, sized each frame from how far it moved, so slow moves stay sharp). There are no sound effects: music and narration only. The hunt's question, "Wizard", "Secure by design", the closing statement and the tagline type in letter by letter: each letter pops in larger, nudged to the right and soft, then settles. The question and the security card soften what is behind them, as a focus pull.

## Method and limits

Motion is measured on the encoded MP4 by phase correlation of consecutive frames at 640 × 360 (3 px steps at 1080p); camera numbers come from `assets/screen.json` and, for revision 13, from its keyframes run through its runtime's interpolation and clamp. The stage lock moves capture frames by whole page pixels and never redraws them. Line lengths and cues for unvoiced lines are estimates; after voicing, RF-05 must be re-run with the real lengths and any line or `until` step moved to its cue. No ASR or human listening pass.

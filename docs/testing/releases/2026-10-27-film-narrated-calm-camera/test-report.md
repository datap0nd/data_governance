## Method and limits

Motion is measured on the encoded MP4 by phase correlation of consecutive frames, for the whole frame at 640 × 360 and for three horizontal bands at 960 × 540 (2–3 px steps at 1080p); camera numbers come from `assets/screen.json` and, for revision 13, from its keyframes run through its runtime's interpolation and clamp. The stage lock and the glides move capture frames by whole page pixels and never redraw them; a glide fills the area its nearer frame leaves open from the neighbouring frame, and keeps the resting pointer from the nearer frame.
